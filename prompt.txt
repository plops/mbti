Ti and Fe (or Fi) can never be your primary and secondary functions because your brain requires structural balance to function. In MBTI theory, your cognitive function stack acts like a psychological gyroscope. To prevent your personality from being completely lopsided, the human mind operates on two fundamental laws of balance: Orientation Balance and Judgment/Perception Balance.
Here is the exact structural breakdown of why these specific combinations are impossible.
Law 1: The Extraverted / Introverted Balance
Your top two functions must alternate between the internal world (Introverted) and the external world (Extraverted).
If you were to lead with two introverted functions (like Ti and Fi simultaneously), your psyche would completely lock itself inside your own head. You would have no way to gather data from reality or interact with the outside world.

    Why Ti and Fi can't pair: Both are deep, internal filters. A person with both as their primary and secondary functions would be caught in an endless loop of evaluating things based on internal logic ("Does this make sense to me?") and internal values ("How do I feel about this?") without ever taking action or observing external facts.

Law 2: The Rational / Irrational Balance
Every human needs a way to take in information (Perceiving) and a way to make decisions about that information (Judging).

    Perceiving Functions (Information Gatherers): Intuition (N) and Sensing (S)
    Judging Functions (Decision Makers): Thinking (T) and Feeling (F)

Your top two functions must consist of one Judging function and one Perceiving function. If your primary function is a Judging function, your secondary must be a Perceiving function to feed it data.

    Why Ti and Fe/Fi can't pair: Ti, Fi, and Fe are all Judging functions. They are machines that make decisions and evaluate data. If you paired Ti with Fe or Fi in your top two spots, your brain would have two powerful decision-making engines but zero ways to gather information. You would be making judgments out of thin air without actually perceiving reality through Sensing (S) or Intuition (N).

The Dynamic Axes (How They Actually Pair)
Because of these laws, functions are locked onto mandatory axes. They exist on a psychological seesaw:

[Dominant Judging] <====== Must be balanced by ======> [Auxiliary Perceiving]
    Ti or Fi (Introverted)                                Ne or Se (Extraverted)

Because Ti is an Introverted Judging function, it demands an Extraverted Perceiving copilot (Ne or Se) to feed it raw, objective observations from the outside world. Only later, down in the 3rd or 4th slot of the stack, can its opposite counterpart (Fe) show up to provide structural balance.
